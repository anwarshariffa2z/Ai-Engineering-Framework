# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community. Participate in good faith, assume positive intent, and critique ideas rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and sharing another person’s private information without permission.

If you experience or observe a violation, report it privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be handled discreetly, investigated fairly, and may result in a warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent ban.
